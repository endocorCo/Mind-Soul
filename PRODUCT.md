# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective therapy clients of Psic. Elizabeth Valencia Cedillo in Texcoco, Estado de México: parents looking for care for children or adolescents, adults seeking individual therapy, and couples. They typically arrive from a shared link (WhatsApp, social media, word of mouth), on a phone, deciding in under a minute whether to trust this person enough to reach out. Many are first-time therapy seekers, so tone matters as much as information.

## Product Purpose

A single-page marketing site that establishes trust and credibility for Elizabeth's private practice, communicates her humanist approach, services and pricing, and converts visitors into a WhatsApp booking. Success = a visitor understands who she is, what she offers, what it costs, and taps through to WhatsApp with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Premium, warm, and professional. Minimalist black/white/gray base with a single, deliberate red accent used only for emphasis (primary CTA, key numbers, active states) — never as a dominant surface color. Calm and unhurried tone ("un espacio para escucharte, comprenderte y crecer"), not clinical or corporate-cold, not playful or juvenile.

## Anti-references

- Do not change the established color palette (white / gray-light / gray / gray-dark / black / red accent #E53935). Any future design work should work within these tokens, not introduce new colors.
- Avoid the generic "therapist template" look: leaf/hand icons, pastel washes, stock-photo warmth clichés.
- Avoid cold corporate/clinical aesthetics (hospital-like, impersonal).

## Design Principles

1. Red is punctuation, not paint — one primary action or accent per view, never a dominant surface.
2. Black and white/gray-light carry the page; dark sections (footer, featured, final CTA) are used sparingly for rhythm, not by default.
3. Mobile is the primary surface — most visitors arrive on a phone from a shared link, so mobile layout gets first-class treatment (no cramped stacks, no excessive scrolling), not a shrunk desktop layout.
4. Every claim on the page must be real information the practitioner provided — no fabricated credentials, testimonials, or contact details.
5. Calm, unhurried, human copy over clinical or salesy language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG level required, but prioritize real-world legibility: strong text contrast, comfortable font sizes, and readable line lengths, especially for an audience that skews toward less tech-fluent and older users reading on a phone. Avoid low-contrast gray-on-white for anything that must actually be read (body copy, prices, hours), reserving lighter grays for decorative or truly secondary elements.
